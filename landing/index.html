How Bideey works

Three things, done consistently, every week.

First

Capture what matters

Record prospects, client opportunities, referrals, tenders and the relationship context behind them — without turning business development into an administrative burden.

Then

Keep the next action visible

Every live opportunity has an owner, a stage, a relationship strength, a next action and a follow-up date. Reminders surface what needs attention before opportunities quietly go cold.

Over time

Learn what produces revenue

See what is moving, what is stalling, which sources convert, which practices carry opportunity, and whether consistent activity is actually leading to instructions.

Why it fits African law firms

Built for relationship-led growth.

The African professional-services market is sophisticated and relationship-rich — it doesn't need generic software, it needs tools shaped to how work actually arrives.

→

Partner-led rainmaking, where a handful of relationships carry a disproportionate share of the firm's revenue.

→

Referral-driven instructions from accountants, bankers, auditors and other professionals.

→

Tenders and panel appointments as a serious, recurring revenue channel.

→

Cross-practice opportunities inside clients the firm already serves.

→

Mobile-first working habits — much of this happens between meetings, not at a desk.

→

Firms operating across multiple sectors, and increasingly multiple jurisdictions.

→

The need for a system simple enough that teams will actually keep it up to date.

→

Local currency presentation from day one, in Kenyan shillings, with further currencies planned.
