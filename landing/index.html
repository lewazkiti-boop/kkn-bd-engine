Multitenant by design

Every firm gets its own private workspace.

Bideey is built so users do not choose from a public list of firms. The first person creates the firm workspace, then invites colleagues through private links.

One user, one firm Accounts are tied to a single firm workspace.
Invite-only access New users join through firm-generated invite links.
Firm-scoped data Pipeline, clients, tenders, referrals, and settings stay inside that firm.
